About

Developer by practice, AI student by training

I'm a final-stage B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence student at Shanghai University of Engineering Science. On the web side, I work in the JavaScript ecosystem: React on the front end, Node.js and Express.js on the server, and MongoDB for data.

My coursework covers machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and medical AI. My thesis tests how well lightweight neural networks diagnose heart conditions from ECG signals when they meet data from a different hospital.
